# Whisk concept / photography shot list

The prototype is at `/concept/`. All boxes marked PHOTO NEEDED are production briefs, not final imagery. Use real photographs only. Existing menu entries are samples: Cindy should confirm the actual opening products before baking specifically for this shoot. No equipment purchase is necessary.

Use a modern phone's normal 1× camera, clean the lens, avoid portrait blur and filters, and keep full-resolution originals. Take a few wider frames as well as the intended crop. Tap the bright trailer to keep chrome highlights from losing all detail. Never stare into reflected sun. Check reflections for people, license plates and private information before approving a frame.

## H01 — trailer in its environment — REQUIRED

- Subject: Cindy's actual polished trailer, outdoors; this is currently the wide slot in the final blue section and a candidate for the eventual hero.
- Orientation / ratio: landscape 3:2; also take a wider landscape version and a separate portrait 3:4 frame for mobile.
- Camera: waist height, approximately 5–8 metres away; adjust until the whole trailer and wheels fit.
- Composition: trailer on the right, filling about 60% of the width. Keep the left third simple sky/grass for potential website headings. In the portrait version, centre the trailer and retain sky above it.
- Light: clear, bright day; try morning or late-afternoon direct sun. Change your angle slightly to find one bright highlight rather than a completely white metal body.
- Background: open blue sky, grass foreground, real trees at a distance. Avoid parked cars and signs.
- Reflections: blue sky across the upper body, grass and trees across the lower body; keep these visible rather than correcting them to gray.
- Service window: open, counter tidy.
- Props: actual signage and packaging if available; no fabricated products or props required.
- Text space: left third and a little sky above the trailer. Photograph wider than you think necessary.

## M01–M04 — menu photographs — REQUIRED once products are confirmed

There are four distinct slots, currently named Morning bun (M01), Chocolate chunk cookie (M02), Lemon loaf (M03), and Scone (M04). These names come from the existing sample menu; they are not an approved product list. Apply the following instructions to each confirmed replacement.

- Subject: one real baked item for each slot, made by Cindy. M01: show folded/spiralled surface; M02: show the cookie edge and chocolate; M03: one slice with cut crumb visible; M04: show the scone's broken or cut interior if appropriate.
- Orientation / ratio: landscape 4:3 master. Preserve enough space to crop to portrait 3:4 on phones.
- Camera: roughly 30–50 cm above the tabletop, 40–70 cm from the item, angled down about 45 degrees. Avoid the phone's ultrawide lens.
- Composition: food centred, filling about 60% of the frame; don't cut off its distinctive shape. Keep the central half useful for the narrower mobile crop.
- Light: natural window light from the side or bright outdoor shade; no flash.
- Background: plain actual packaging paper or an uncluttered counter.
- Reflections: not required; avoid shiny props that distract from the food.
- Service window: not applicable; no trailer needs to be visible.
- Props: the item, plain paper, optionally a knife for the loaf. Remove unrelated crockery and branding.
- Text space: no text overlays. Preserve a small clean border around the food; labels sit below the photo.

## D01 — bake at the chrome counter — NICE TO HAVE

- Subject: one real baked item on paper at the trailer's service counter.
- Orientation / ratio: landscape 3:2.
- Camera: just above counter height, approximately 50–90 cm away.
- Composition: item slightly right of centre; counter and its reflection fill the lower third.
- Light: daylight with one controlled white highlight on the metal; keep the bake out of deep shadow.
- Background: service-window interior, tidied but natural.
- Reflections: visible blue sky or green grass in the counter/body, and a soft real reflection of the paper or bake.
- Service window: open.
- Props: one bake, real packaging paper, optional approved Whisk packaging.
- Text space: none over the photo; leave a little breathing room on the left for crop flexibility.

## C01 — Cindy at work — OPTIONAL, no current homepage slot

- Subject: Cindy working at the service window, with her permission.
- Orientation / ratio: portrait 4:5 plus a landscape 3:2 alternative.
- Camera: eye/chest height, 2–3 metres away.
- Composition: Cindy slightly off centre, hands and a section of chrome visible.
- Light/background: natural daylight, actual trailer interior; avoid a posed corporate portrait.
- Reflections: show a little outdoor environment without distracting reflected faces.
- Service window: open.
- Props: actual baking/serving tools and products being used.
- Text space: no overlay required; keep room above the head and around hands.
