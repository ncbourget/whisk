Menu & order

Good to know.

Ordering, pickup, and ingredients.

How do I order?

When online ordering is open, follow an item’s Order on Square link, or use our Square shop if available. You’ll review the order and pay on Square. This website never asks for card details.

Where and when do I pick up?

Pickup details will be shared when ordering opens. Please check the date, time, and location before paying.

How far ahead should I order?

Order deadlines will be listed with each menu release.

Can I order several things together?

If our Square shop is linked, add your items to the basket there. Individual payment links may create separate orders. Check your pickup details before paying.

What if I have a food allergy?

Ingredient and allergen details will be confirmed before ordering opens. If you have a food allergy, please contact Cindy before ordering. Dietary labels do not guarantee the absence of cross-contact.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Cancellation and refund details will be posted before we begin taking orders.

How do I know my order went through?

Look for Square’s confirmation and receipt. If checkout fails or you’re unsure whether you paid, check for a receipt and contact Cindy before trying again. A visit to this website is not proof of payment.

How should I store my baked goods?

Look for storage notes in each menu item’s details, or ask Cindy when you collect your order.

Does this website use tracking cookies?

We haven’t added advertising trackers, analytics, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may keep standard security logs. Square and social platforms have their own privacy policies when you visit their websites.
