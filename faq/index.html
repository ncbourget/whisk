Good to know.

Ordering, pickup, and ingredients.

How do I order?

Ordering is not open yet. You can try the cart on Whisk; no order is sent and no payment is taken. Once connected, checkout will send your complete order to Square for payment and pickup confirmation.

Where and when do I pick up?

Pickup details will be shared when ordering opens. Please check the date, time, and location before paying.

How far ahead should I order?

Pre-orders require at least 3 days before pickup. You may pre-order up to a week in advance. Everyday stand items are not available to order online.

Can I order several things together?

Yes. Add items to your Whisk cart, then review everything together. Checkout is not open yet. Pickup availability and preparation time will be confirmed before payment.

What if I have a food allergy?

As a lifelong allergy sufferer, I understand the concern — and the disappointment — of seeing something that looks delicious but may be too risky to eat. I always clearly label allergens, and I never cook, bake, or use anything in my home kitchen that could cause cross-contamination. Utensils, bowls, pans, and surfaces are kept strictly separate. I also bake from a shared commercial kitchen. While I follow careful cleaning practices, I can’t fully control what others prepare in that space. I share this openly so you can make the best decision for your needs. Your safety matters to me.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Cancellation and refund details will be posted before we begin taking orders.

How do I know my order went through?

Look for Square’s confirmation and receipt. If checkout fails or you’re unsure whether you paid, check for a receipt and contact Cindy before trying again. A visit to this website is not proof of payment.

How should I store my baked goods?

Look for storage notes in each menu item’s details, or ask Cindy when you collect your order.

Does this website use tracking cookies?

We haven’t added advertising trackers, analytics, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may keep standard security logs. Square and social platforms have their own privacy policies when you visit their websites.
