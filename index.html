Meet Cindy.

About Cindy — Baker & Founder of Whisk

I’m Cindy, the baker behind Whisk. Baking has always been my happy place — the quiet moment when chocolate melts smoothly in a pan, or when the scent of vanilla fills the kitchen and everything feels a little calmer. I’ve been baking for more than 20 years, sharing treats with friends, coworkers, and anyone who loves a good homemade dessert.

During the pandemic, baking became my outlet. I experimented with new recipes, new techniques, and just about every sweet idea that crossed my mind. Without colleagues to share my creations with, my kitchen became my test kitchen — and yes, I gained 20 pounds in the process. Worth it.

Fast-forward six years. After nearly three decades in the technology world, I took an early retirement package and decided it was time to bring my long‑imagined dream to life. Whisk is that dream — a traveling bakery trailer offering everyday treats, special‑occasion orders, goodies for your office, holiday pies, and custom desserts for just about anything you can think of.

Whisk is about joy, comfort, and connection. It’s about bringing homemade goodness to the places and people I love. And I can’t wait to share it with you.
