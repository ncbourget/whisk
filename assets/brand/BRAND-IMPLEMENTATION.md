# Whisk final identity

Source: Meredith's `WHISK - FINAL BRAND FILES/Whisk - Brand Guidelines.pdf`.

The active storefront and content notebook load `assets/css/identity.css` after their layout styles. Quincy CF Regular supplies display headings; Bryant Regular/Medium/Bold supplies body text, navigation, labels, and controls. Fonts are self-hosted from the supplied OTF files. These supplied commercial brand fonts are not covered by the older fonts' OFL licenses.

Primary ink is Deep Blue (#00365D), with White surfaces, Sand Dune (#E7DFC6) section panels and Whisk Blue (#91AEDB) editor highlights. Whisk Blue also fills the storefront footer; Willow Green highlights short hero/location captions. Apricot Cream with Espresso Bean lettering identifies pre-order cross-links. Sand Dune remains the filter and information-panel surface. These roles keep all seven palette colors present, with dark text on light backgrounds; mobile retains the Willow location caption when the hero caption is hidden. Existing torn-paper boundaries, product layouts, and .5pt underlines remain.

The full and condensed logos are lossless WebP exports of Meredith's transparent PNGs, retaining their original aspect ratio and artwork. The supplied SVGs contain live LiebeLotte text, but that font is not included; use these raster exports until Meredith supplies outlined SVGs. Do not substitute another font inside a logo.

Only selected production assets in `assets/brand/final/` are copied by the build; the entire source brand package is not deployed. Frozen snapshots keep their own assets. Menu data, ordering rules, and administrative authorization are unchanged.
